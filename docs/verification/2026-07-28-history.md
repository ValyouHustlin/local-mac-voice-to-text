## Isolated live app

Launch command:

```text
.build/debug/parrot run --skip-doctor --no-overlay \
  --data-directory /tmp/parrot-history-verify.sC8fs7
```

Observed:

```text
loading whisper-base.en...
✓ whisper-base.en ready
listening on fn hold · model: whisper-base.en · ^C to quit
```

The temporary database contained three deliberately non-private fixture
records: two inserted and one failed insertion. No real transcript history was
read or changed.

Gestures and observed results:

1. Clicked `Transcript History…` in the Parrot menu. A native, resizable split
   window opened with three newest-first rows, immediate detail selection,
   timestamps, target applications, model/language, audio duration,
   transcription duration, and insertion status.
2. Entered `failed` in Search. The list changed to `1 match`, selected the
   failed record, showed `Not inserted`, and displayed the preserved failure
   reason.
3. Clicked Copy. The pasteboard contained exactly
   `This transcript survived a failed insertion and stayed recoverable.` The
   verification script restored the pre-existing pasteboard record afterward.
4. Clicked Correct. `Add Dictionary Correction` opened with the complete
   transcript prefilled in Heard as. Cancel left the dictionary unchanged.
5. Opened a blank TextEdit document, opened History from that target, searched
   for the failed record, and clicked Reinsert. History hid, TextEdit
   reactivated, and the full transcript appeared in the blank document. The
   document was closed without saving.
6. Clicked Delete and confirmed. The filtered result changed to `0 matches`,
   and SQLite reported two remaining records.
7. Clicked Clear All, first chose Cancel and observed the records remain, then
   confirmed in the final isolated pass. SQLite reported zero remaining
   records.
8. Stopped and relaunched Parrot after inserting a recoverable fixture record,
   clicked its Dock item, searched `failed`, and observed `1 match` with
   `Restart should preserve this failed transcript.` in the detail view.

The inspected UI is captured in

## Dock and icon

- The application now runs with regular macOS activation policy while retaining
  its menu bar item.
- System Events listed `parrot` as an application Dock item.
- Clicking the Dock item with no visible windows opened Transcript History.
- The vector-derived icon was rendered and visually inspected at 1024, 128, 64,
  and 32 pixels. The bird remained clear at 32 pixels.
- The inspected SVG is embedded directly in the executable. A compiled probe
  ran `AppIdentity.configure`, read back `NSApplication.applicationIconImage`
  as a 1024-pixel image, and matched the inspected icon. This preserves the
  existing single-executable install and release paths.

The exact custom image was not captured inside Aaron's auto-hidden live Dock;
Dock presence and click behavior were observed, while the asset itself was
inspected from the same embedded SVG.

## Still required for the full P3 exit gate

- Create history records through fresh microphone dictation rather than
  isolated fixtures.
- Deliberately force a live insertion failure and observe the coordinator-created
  failed record after restart.
- Repeat the microphone-created record through the native, browser, and
  Electron verification targets required by the lane contract.

Until those observations exist, the history feature is implemented and usable,
but its full live-audio exit receipt remains open.
